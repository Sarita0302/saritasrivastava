01
✦ Data Integrity · Listings Management · Enterprise SaaS

Experience Accuracy Score

A real-time listing data verification and health scoring system for enterprise multi-location businesses

View Live Prototype
The Problem

Enterprise businesses manage listings across 100+ directories — Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Facebook, Yelp, and more. The platform publishes data. But nobody verifies it actually landed correctly — or stayed correct after external overrides. The result is a 20% data inconsistency rate and 8% ingestion failure rate, while customers trust a green dashboard that is quietly lying to them. This is False Confidence — the platform shows success, the customer experiences failure.

The Solution

EAS is a composite 0–100 health score per listing measuring accuracy, consistency, and verification across all connected directories. The Closed-Loop System: Verify → Detect → Prioritise → Resolve → Re-verify → Badge.

🔍Real-time verification engine — monitors data changes across distributed external sources
⚡Root cause classification — ingestion failure vs external override vs propagation delay
📊Priority-ranked dashboard — locations ranked by EAS score with one-click resolution
🏅Verified Listing Badge — earned at 80+ EAS sustained for 7 days, shown in search results
🤖AI search connection — high EAS = accurate AI citations in ChatGPT and Perplexity

02
✦ AI · Voice-First · Emotional Wellness · Consumer

IMH — I'm Here

A voice-first AI reflection companion that listens, detects emotional patterns, and shows weekly insights in human language — not charts

View Live Prototype
The Problem

People have thoughts, stress, and emotions they cannot always express. Journaling has a 95% drop-off rate — it requires effort, time, and discipline that most people cannot sustain. Talking helps, but a safe, always-available space does not exist. Therapy has too high a vulnerability barrier. The result: emotions remain unprocessed, stress accumulates, and self-awareness never develops.

The Differentiator

IMH is positioned as a witness, not a solution. The core insight: instead of mood charts, users see their week in human language — "This week felt more stressed than calm. Work-related stress appeared four times. There were small positive moments on two days." That is the real differentiator — insight over data.

🎙️Voice-first — 2 to 5 minutes of speaking, no account required, no recording stored
💬Gentle reflection prompts — not advice, not therapy. Just the right question at the right moment
🌡️Emotional detection — five states: Calm, Neutral, Stressed, Low, Overwhelmed. No clinical labels
📅Weekly summary in human language — the real differentiator over every competitor
⚠️Safety layer — gentle nudge after 5+ days of persistent distress, with user-controlled options
🔒Full user controls — view history, delete entries, turn off tracking anytime

Identity shift, isolation, cannot burden family

Shrinking social circle, thoughts with nowhere to go

✦ PM Toolkit · Prioritisation · Frameworks · AI-Native

RICE Prioritisation Engine

An interactive product prioritisation tool that scores features using the RICE framework and generates intelligent roadmap recommendations with PM insights

View Live Prototype
What It Does

The RICE Prioritisation Engine transforms backlog management from opinion-based debates into evidence-based decisions. Input any feature — name, description, and four RICE parameters — and the tool instantly calculates the score, ranks your full roadmap, generates PM insights, and classifies each feature into the priority matrix (Ship Now, Plan Carefully, Quick Win, or Deprioritise).

The Formula

RICE = ( Reach × Impact × Confidence ) ÷ Effort

📥Feature input with name, description, and four slider-based RICE parameters
🏆Live ranked roadmap — sorted by score, with colour-coded health indicators
💡AI-generated PM insight — identifies the weakest assumption and calls out low-confidence items
📐Priority matrix — effort vs impact quadrant for visual decision-making
📊Summary stats — total features, average score, total effort, top score
🗑️Full backlog management — add, remove, and clear features dynamically
Why I Built This

Most PMs use RICE. Few understand it well enough to build it. I built this to close that gap — and to make the difference between knowing a framework and owning one visible in real time.
